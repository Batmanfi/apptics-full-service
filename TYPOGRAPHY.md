# Typography inventory

Computed from the deployed HTML/CSS at a 1440px desktop viewport and a 390px mobile viewport. Values are in CSS pixels. Mobile means 600px and below; tablet styles at 601–800px and small-desktop styles at 801–1100px have intermediate sizes. All visible page text uses Geist. The inherited Geist Mono on the audience wrapper is overridden by Geist on its text.

All H1 and H2 headings are 48px/58px on desktop and 28px/36px on mobile. Heading underlines are removed. All bullet lists use 18px/27px desktop and 16px/25px at 800px and below, with 14px between items.
