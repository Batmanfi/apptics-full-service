Frequently
asked questions

What types of businesses does Apptics support?

Brands on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop, BigCommerce and sticky.io, in standard and high-risk verticals: CBD, supplements, vape, nutra, adult, weight loss, firearms accessories and subscription boxes. If Stripe, PayPal or Shopify Payments rejected you or shut you off after years of clean processing, Apptics works with brands in your position.

How fast can I get approved?

Most merchants get approved within 72 hours. We use that time to understand your business: your chargeback history, refund policy, vertical and growth plans. That review is what keeps you from getting shut off later. A processor that approves you in minutes hasn't looked at your business, and that's usually the one that freezes you once your volume grows. Once you're approved, our team moves your processing over in 5 to 7 business days with no dev work on your side.

What if Apptics freezes me the way Stripe did?

Your account runs across 40+ banking partners. If one bank's risk appetite shifts, your volume moves to another one. A person underwrites you before you migrate, so you won't get a 120-day hold because a computer got spooked.

How are you different from Stripe or PayPal?

Stripe and PayPal put dentists, SaaS companies, restaurants and your ecom brand through the same underwriting model. Apptics works with ecom brands and nothing else, so every bank relationship and support process is set up for how you run.

Do you offer global payment processing?

Yes. Apptics supports 180+ currencies and processes in North America, Europe, the UK and APAC, with local acquiring where it's available. Local acquiring cuts the cross-border declines generalist processors leave you with.

Can I integrate with my existing stack?

Yes. Apptics plugs into Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop, BigCommerce, sticky.io and 50+ other carts, CRMs and checkouts. Our team handles the migration 1:1 and you don't need a developer.

How do you handle chargebacks and fraud?

Chargeback Guard fights disputes for you, and we train our fraud screening on ecom patterns. Merchants above certain volume tiers also get direct Visa and Mastercard liaison support to protect them from the MATCH list.
