Frequently
Asked Questions

What types of businesses does Apptics support?

Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop, BigCommerce, and sticky.io merchants across standard and high-risk verticals — CBD, supplements, vape, nutra, adult, weight loss, firearms accessories, and subscription boxes of every kind. If you've been rejected by Stripe, PayPal, or Shopify Payments, or if you've been disabled after years of clean processing, Apptics is built for exactly your situation.

How fast can I get approved?

Most merchants are pre-approved in days, not weeks. Underwriting happens with a real person who reviews your business in context — including your chargeback history, refund policy, vertical, and growth trajectory. Once approved, full migration typically completes in 5–7 business days with zero dev hours on your side.

What if Apptics freezes me the way Stripe did?

This is the question every operator asks — and it's exactly why Apptics is built on multi-rail processing across 40+ banking partners instead of a single acquirer. You're underwritten with full context before you migrate, so there are no surprises later. If one banking partner's risk appetite ever shifts, your account rebalances automatically to another rail. No single point of failure. No 120-day hold because a computer got spooked.

How are you different from Stripe or PayPal?

Stripe and PayPal are generalist processors built to serve every business type through one underwriting model — dentists, SaaS, restaurants, and your eCom brand, all in the same risk bucket. Apptics is built specifically for eCom. Every banking relationship, every approval pathway, every support workflow is tuned to how eCom businesses actually operate. Same service category on paper. Completely different infrastructure underneath.

Do you offer global payment processing?

Yes. Apptics supports 180+ currencies and processes across North America, Europe, the UK, and APAC, with local acquiring where available. Global reach without the cross-border decline rates generalist processors charge you for.

Can I integrate with my existing stack?

Yes. Apptics plugs into every major shopping cart, CRM, subscription platform, and checkout system — Shopify, Woo, Magento, PrestaShop, BigCommerce, sticky.io, and 50+ others. Migration is handled 1:1 by our team. Zero dev hours required from your side.

How do you handle chargebacks and fraud?

Built-in Chargeback Guard that fights disputes on your behalf, plus fraud screening trained on eCom-specific patterns — not generic business signals. For merchants above certain volume tiers, we also provide direct VISA and Mastercard liaison support to protect against MATCH list exposure.
