Frequently asked questions
about our services.

What types of businesses does Apptics Pay support?

Apptics Pay supports eCommerce, subscription, high-volume, and hard-to-place merchants across a wide range of categories. If you have outgrown Stripe, Shopify Payments, or PayPal, been hit with reserves, or need a processor that understands your business model, we can help you find the right merchant account.

How fast can I get approved?

Most qualified merchants can get reviewed within a few business days. Once approved, our team helps you connect your store, checkout, or payment gateway so you can start processing as quickly as possible.

What happens if I have been shut down before?

That is one of the main reasons merchants come to Apptics Pay. We review your business upfront, look at your processing history, risk profile, chargebacks, refunds, and offer, then match you with the right banking partner instead of forcing you into a one-size-fits-all processor.

How is Apptics Pay different from Stripe, Shopify Payments, or PayPal?

Stripe, Shopify Payments, and PayPal work well until your business no longer fits their risk model. Apptics Pay is built for merchants who need real underwriting, flexible processing options, chargeback protection, and support from people who understand eCommerce risk.

How much is Apptics Pay?

Pricing depends on your business model, processing volume, industry, risk profile, and the merchant account you are approved for. Our team reviews your current setup and gives you clear pricing before you switch.

What if Apptics freezes me the way Stripe or Shopify Payments did?

Apptics Pay is built to reduce that risk before you start processing. Your business is reviewed upfront by real underwriting, so your merchant account is matched to your actual risk profile. The goal is to avoid surprise shutdowns, reserves, and holds by placing you with the right processing partner from day one.

Do you support high-risk merchants?

Yes. Apptics Pay works with many high-volume and hard-to-place merchants. Approval depends on your product category, chargeback history, refund policy, fulfillment model, and processing volume, but we are built to help businesses that general processors often reject or restrict.

Can I use Apptics Pay with my current store or checkout?

Yes. Apptics Pay can connect with major eCommerce platforms, custom checkouts, and payment gateways like NMI and Authorize.net. Our team helps with integration so you don’t have to rebuild your stack just to start processing.

Do I have to leave Shopify?

No. Many merchants use Apptics Pay while still running their store on Shopify. The goal is to give you more control over payment processing, not force you to rebuild your business.

How do you handle chargebacks and fraud?

Apptics Pay merchants are eligible to enroll into Apptics Shield which helps catch disputes early through real-time Visa and Mastercard alerts, then can trigger refunds before they turn into chargebacks. This helps protect your processor relationship, reduce chargeback rates, and lower the risk of frozen payouts, reserves, or account shutdowns.

Do you offer global payment processing?

Apptics Pay can support merchants across multiple regions and currencies depending on your business, location, and approval. Our team will review your setup and help route you toward the best available processing options.

Who is Apptics Pay best for?

Apptics Pay is best for merchants doing real volume who need more stability than Stripe, Shopify Payments, or PayPal can offer. If payment holds, reserves, chargebacks, or processor risk are slowing you down, Apptics Pay is built for you.
