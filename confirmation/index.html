01

What exactly is Apptics?

We are an ecommerce SaaS company that helps established ecommerce brands to double their MRR using customizable checkout flows, subscription infrastructure, supported payment processing options, and chargeback protection.

The team audits your current setup, recommends the components that make economic sense, integrates them with the store, and supports ongoing optimization. It is not simply another checkout app, payment processor, or chargeback dashboard.

02

Is Apptics suitable for a brand like ours?

Apptics is primarily built for established ecommerce and retail brands, generating more than $50,000 per month, that already know how to acquire customers and want to improve what happens after the click.

The strongest fit is usually a brand with a measurable opportunity in at least one of these areas:
- checkout value,
- subscription conversion,
- recurring-payment continuity,
- processor resilience, or chargeback reduction.

03

Is Apptics a payment processor?

Apptics is not a single standalone processor. Our system routes transactions intelligently across 35+ banking relationships. When one processor’s risk model shifts, your account keeps processing through another rail.

04

Do we need Shopify Plus or a completely new stack?

No. Apptics checkout flows do not require Shopify Plus, and an implementation does not automatically mean replacing every tool in the current stack.

The Revenue Audit identifies what should remain, what should change, and whether the rollout should happen in stages. A stable component should not be replaced simply to make the stack look more uniform.

05

What happens to our existing subscribers?

Apptics can tokenize and vault eligible subscriber payment credentials so the recurring-revenue asset is less dependent on a single processor.

However, existing subscribers are not automatically portable. Token compatibility, processor rules, consent requirements, gateway support, and the current subscription setup must be reviewed before any migration.

06

How long does setup take?

There is no honest universal timeline. It depends on the components selected, the current stack, data and access availability, integration complexity, processor underwriting, and whether subscriber migration is involved.

Setting up payments can take as low as 48 days.

During the Revenue Audit, the team will map the likely timeline, dependencies, and required people for the prospect’s specific setup.

08

How does the 90 day MRR guarantee work?

The guarantee is not a blanket promise that applies to every store. Eligibility is established during the audit.

Before implementation, Apptics and the client document the starting MRR baseline, the 90-day measurement period, required actions, attribution method, exclusions, and the platform fees covered by the guarantee. If the agreed MRR target is not reached and the documented conditions have been met, the applicable platform fees are returned according to those written terms.

09

What is the genuine worst case?

An implementation can disappoint when the product lacks a legitimate subscription use case, token or processor compatibility is limited, underwriting does not produce the required approval, the migration is rushed, or checkout changes lift gross AOV while hurting margin, refunds, or customer experience.
